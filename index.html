How these numbers are worked out
  • Scheduled: the customers who were active and had that weekday as their visit day on that date, taken from the dated schedule history, added up over the days. Every rep shares the same list. Customers with no visit day yet are never counted.
  • Visited: scheduled customers this rep recorded at least once that day. A repeat visit is kept in the history but counted once.
  • Still due = Scheduled − Visited.
  • Orders (visits) / No order: every recorded visit with that outcome, including repeat visits and shops that were not scheduled that day.
  • Manager-entered orders: orders a manager entered on the rep’s behalf. They are credited to the rep here, but they are not shop visits and are not in Visited, Coverage, Conversion or the visit lists.
  • Coverage = Visited ÷ Scheduled. Conversion = Orders (visits) ÷ all recorded visits. Shown as “—” when the divisor is zero or when no date in the range has a reliable schedule.
  • Schedule changes: a change applies from the start of the Malawi day on which it was saved (server time) and never alters earlier days. Dates before the schedule history began have no reliable record, so no target or percentage is calculated for them and the report says so.
  • Date range: up to 366 days (both end dates counted). A longer range is refused, never shortened, so every figure covers the same dates. The line “Showing…” above the table states the dates the report covers.
